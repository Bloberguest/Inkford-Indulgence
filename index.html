Our Story

Ten years of
Italian inspiration

Inkford Indulgence began with a lesson that changed everything.

Ten years ago, Mark, the owner, was taught by an Italian man whose family had been making ice cream for generations. He passed on the knowledge and craft of making authentic artisan ice cream — and the rest is history.

Today, Inkford Indulgence brings that traditional know-how to every batch, creating flavours that are made to be enjoyed, shared and remembered.
